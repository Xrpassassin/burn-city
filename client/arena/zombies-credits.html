Modifications and provenance

Model exports were independently prepared and optimized by Dernière Nuit à Perpignan, whose source credits identify the original Sketchfab and OpenGameArt artists, source downloads and conversions. WebP material compression, model scaling, root-motion correction, animation aliasing, gameplay-aware appearance selection and hit/death visual fallback are changes made by that project and/or BURN City. The original creator, source and license remain cited for each character.
