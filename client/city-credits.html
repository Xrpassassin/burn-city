City citizens (3D people)

The people walking around BURN City are based on these 3D models from Sketchfab. Changes: the meshes were simplified for phones, textures were resized and re-encoded, a humanoid skeleton was added (with hand and thigh skin weights separated), and original walk and idle animations, per-person gaits and idle gestures were created in-house.

Player avatar

The shared player character in BURN City and its games is based on this 3D model from Sketchfab. Changes: the mesh was simplified from 1,976,719 triangles to 9,000 (close-up) and 3,848 (other players at a distance), keeping the original texture seams, textures were resized from 4K to 1024 (512 on phones) and re-encoded, a humanoid skeleton was added, original Idle, Walk, Run, Ride, Sit and Jump clips were created in-house, and a headless first-person body was exported. The gas mask and silhouette were preserved.

  • "Gas Mask Guy" by The Creator, licensed under CC BY 4.0

Spaceflight preview

The CB1 ship and International Space Station that orbit over BURN City and sit on the airport launch pad (and fly the in-City docking trip) are based on these 3D models from Sketchfab. Changes: Earth, cables and astronauts were removed from the station; meshes were simplified for phones to 7,426 triangles (CB1 close-up) and 9,892 (ISS close-up) with lower LODs of 2,448 and 2,999; textures were resized to 1024 (512 on phones) and re-encoded as WebP; and the models were scaled and oriented to the preview docking frame. A procedural stand-in is used if a model cannot load.

  • "SPACESHIP - CB1" by Kerem Kavalci, licensed under CC BY 4.0
  • "International Space Station" by BlendRuN, licensed under CC BY 4.0

The Ember Bar (BURN Tower)

The bartender and the regular at the Ember Bar are based on these 3D models from Sketchfab. Changes: the meshes were simplified (443,196 and 288,690 triangles down to about 12,000 and 9,000, with extra detail kept on the faces; about 4,800 and 6,500 on phones), textures were resized to 2048 (1024 on phones) and re-encoded as WebP, Blackbeard's blades were removed, a simple humanoid skeleton was added, and the idle, seated, sipping, serving and celebration motion was created in-house. A plain bar is used if a model cannot load.

  • "Pirate Crew" by RYStudio, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — the Ember Bar's bartender
  • "3d model inspired of Blackbeard" by FrandProductions, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — Blackbeard, the regular on the end stool

Street buildings around the towers

The sci-fi street buildings in the BURN, IMM and XRPyro districts are based on these 3D models from Sketchfab. Changes: each model's parts were merged into one mesh, re-centred and scaled to fit the existing city lots, Building 4 (39,054 triangles) and Building 5 (17,648) were simplified to about 5,900 and 4,200 triangles, a low-detail version (about 340 to 690 triangles) was added for distance, textures were resized from 1024 to 512 px and re-encoded as JPEG, the normal and metalness maps are not used, and each copy gets a slight district tint.
