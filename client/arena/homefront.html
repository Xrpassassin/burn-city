🎯 Live siege defense

Call the horde: 2–4 packs march on your walls from 46–58 units out and reach them in roughly 26–40 seconds. Climb a gun platform (walk up its stairs) for range and a clear line of fire over the walls, or crew a mortar pit (refuge level 5) and tap the ground beyond the walls to shell the approach. Party members can reinforce you with the defender link. The server decides every hit.
