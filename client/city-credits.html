Player avatar

The shared player character in BURN City and its games is based on this 3D model from Sketchfab. Changes: the mesh was simplified from 1,976,719 triangles to 9,000 (close-up) and 3,848 (other players at a distance), keeping the original texture seams, textures were resized from 4K to 1024 (512 on phones) and re-encoded, a humanoid skeleton was added, original Idle, Walk, Run, Ride, Sit and Jump clips were created in-house, and a headless first-person body was exported. The gas mask and silhouette were preserved.

  • "Gas Mask Guy" by The Creator, licensed under CC BY 4.0

Hover Mage (optional player model)

The optional Hover Mage body (Settings > Player) is based on this 3D model from Sketchfab. Changes: the mesh was simplified from 450,570 triangles to 15,000 (desktop) and 5,000 (phones and distance), the four source pieces were merged, the flat normal map and spare UV sets were dropped, the base-color texture was resized from 2K to 1024 (512 on phones) and re-encoded as WebP, and the model was scaled and turned to the player frame. In game it floats with a procedural bob, hem sway and lean; no rig or clips were added.

  • This work is based on "Dark Hooded Robe" by maksfrolov947, licensed under CC BY 4.0

Spaceflight preview

The CB1 ship and International Space Station that orbit over BURN City and sit on the airport launch pad (and fly the in-City docking trip) are based on these 3D models from Sketchfab. Changes: Earth, cables and astronauts were removed from the station; meshes were simplified for phones to 7,426 triangles (CB1 close-up) and 9,892 (ISS close-up) with lower LODs of 2,448 and 2,999; textures were resized to 1024 (512 on phones) and re-encoded as WebP; and the models were scaled and oriented to the preview docking frame. A procedural stand-in is used if a model cannot load.

  • "International Space Station" by BlendRuN, licensed under CC BY 4.0

Estates Kenney furniture and yards

Additional furniture, lamps, rugs, plants, fences, paths and yard pieces on Estates Island are based on Kenney’s public-domain (CC0) Furniture Kit and Nature Kit. A small phone-light subset is bundled as self-contained GLBs (furniture was converted from the official OBJ/FBX pack; nature models were already GLB). Kenney asks for optional credit to Kenney or www.kenney.nl. File-level notes and license text: assets/estates-kit/ATTRIBUTION.md.

Owned lots carry a procedural yard flag and a nameplate using the already-public catalog address plus a short wallet tag from the public ownership list. Unsold lots carry a procedural For Sale yard sign (listing look only; no prices or financial details). Kenney’s CC0 City Kit (Suburban) and Building Kit were reviewed for whole-house meshes but not bundled, so collision shells and door positions stay compatible with the door-anchored Enter/Edit chip. Tier identity (silhouette, roof, materials, trim and interior palette) is authored in-engine and dressed with the Kenney furniture and nature pieces above. Walk-in furniture quality scales with how many typoon upgrades a property already has: unsold and show-home tours stay on the basic set, mid counts add plants, art, rugs and lamps from the same CC BY Sketchfab collection, and high counts add chandeliers, brass trim and glass display cases. No prices are shown.

  • Furniture Kit by Kenney, licensed under CC0 1.0 — also on OpenGameArt — rugs, lamps, tables, chairs, plants and screens inside Estates homes
  • Nature Kit by Kenney, licensed under CC0 1.0 — also on OpenGameArt — front-walk paths, low fences, bushes, flowers and rocks in lot setbacks

The Ember Bar (BURN Tower)

The bartender and the regular at the Ember Bar are based on these 3D models from Sketchfab. Changes: the meshes were simplified (443,196 and 288,690 triangles down to about 12,000 and 9,000, with extra detail kept on the faces; about 4,800 and 6,500 on phones), textures were resized to 2048 (1024 on phones) and re-encoded as WebP, Blackbeard's blades were removed, a simple humanoid skeleton was added, and the idle, seated, sipping, serving and celebration motion was created in-house. The Undercroft (Ember Crossroads)

The props below are based on 3D models from Sketchfab; changes: each model was merged into one mesh, re-centred and scaled, the skull was simplified from 1,714 to 790 triangles, textures were resized to 512 px (256 for the skull) and re-encoded as WebP, and the crystal and skull colours are tinted in game.
