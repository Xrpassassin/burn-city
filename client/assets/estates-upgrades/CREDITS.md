# Estates Tycoon upgrade models

Optimized from CC BY 4.0 Sketchfab models with `scripts/estates-upgrade-models/build_upgrade_model.py` (simplified, re-scaled and re-oriented, unused PBR maps removed, colour textures resized to 256 px WebP; the helicopter livery is split into a separate mesh). Raw downloads are not committed. Public credits: `client/city-credits.html`.
