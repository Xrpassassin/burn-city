Presentation

The first four archetypes are original procedural meshes. Slime, Bat, and Dragon are imported animated Quaternius models (CC0 1.0) with adjustable scale, tint, emissive accent, wireframe, and compatible named-part proportions where available. GLB payloads are bundled locally as base64 text and decoded in the workshop; the vendored Three.js r160 GLTF loader keeps model loading isolated from live combat. This page does not change production enemy sprites, combat values, or saves.
