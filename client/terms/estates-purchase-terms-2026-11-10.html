Burn City Estates — Virtual Property Purchase Terms

Version: estates-terms-2026-11-10 · Effective: November 10, 2026

1. What you are buying

An Estates property is a virtual, in-game property in Burn City. Buying one gives the buying wallet in-game access to that lot: its house, interior, decorations and the related gameplay features. Ownership is recorded by the Burn City game server against your XRPL wallet address.

A property is not real estate, not an NFT, not a token, not a share in any company, and not ownership of treasury funds, liquidity-pool (AMM) positions or LP tokens. The treasury keeps every LP token.

2. Price and payment

The price is shown in XRP before you pay. You pay with a Xaman-signed XRP payment to the Estates treasury. A purchase completes only after the payment is validated on the XRP Ledger and matches the quoted amount, buyer and property. Payments are final once validated, except where Burn City chooses to refund a duplicate, late or failed purchase.

3. What happens to the purchase XRP

Burn City keeps a small part of each purchase (currently about 1%) as XRP for network fees and account reserves. The rest is used by Burn City to add liquidity to the IMG/RLUSD pool on the XRP Ledger: roughly half is exchanged for RLUSD and half for IMG, and both are deposited together. If prices move too much, or a deposit would be too large for the pool, the funds stay in the treasury until a later deposit is possible.

4. Holder rewards (no guarantee)

At the end of each completed reward period, Burn City may withdraw only the trading fees that the treasury's pool position earned above the amount deposited. The amount deposited stays in the pool. Rewards are paid from realized pool fee gains when available. The reward period is monthly unless Burn City has enabled daily periods, as shown on the live Estates status page. Any realized amount for a period is split:

  • 10% is used to burn IMG (IMG is sent to its issuer, which removes it from circulation);
  • 10% is added back to the IMG/RLUSD pool;
  • 80% is shared among property holders in proportion to each property's share units.

Holder rewards are paid in IMG and RLUSD to the wallet that owns the property when the split is made. To receive them, that wallet needs IMG and RLUSD trust lines. Amounts too small to send (currently under 0.05 RLUSD in value) and amounts that cannot be delivered are carried forward to a later period.

There is no guaranteed return. Rewards depend on trading activity in the pool and may be zero in any period. Pool prices can fall, and the value of IMG and RLUSD can change. Rewards can be delayed, paused (for example when the treasury safety guard pauses outgoing transactions), changed or stopped. Nothing here promises that you will get back what you paid. Buy a property only for the in-game experience.

5. Resale

If in-game resale is turned on, you may list your property for another player to buy. Rewards follow the wallet that owns the property when each period's split is made.

6. Service

Burn City is a game and is provided as is. Features, artwork, availability and these terms can change. A new version of these terms applies only to purchases made after it is published; each purchase records the terms version and SHA-256 fingerprint that the buyer accepted.

7. Contact

Questions or problems with a purchase: contact Burn City through the in-game support channel or on GitHub at github.com/Xrpassassin/burn-city.
