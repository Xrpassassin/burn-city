Environment props — Kenney (CC0 1.0)

The original Zombies maps use a selection of compressed, instanced low-poly props for wrecked cars, fences, barricades, debris, dumpsters, crates, barrels and flicker lights. These are visual only and do not change collision or gameplay. Models by Kenney, CC0 1.0 (attribution is not required; included with thanks):

  • Car Kit 3.1 — sedan, taxi, van, tire / bumper / door debris
    kenney.nl/assets/car-kit · OpenGameArt
  • Survival Kit 2.0 — wood fences, barrels, crates
    kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit · OpenGameArt
  • Graveyard Kit 5.0 — damaged wood and iron fences, rubble, lamp posts
    kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit · OpenGameArt
  • City Kit (Roads) 2.2 — construction barriers, cones, work fences, work lights, dumpsters
    kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads · OpenGameArt

Files live in assets/env/ with each pack's license text and a full provenance note in ATTRIBUTION.md. Textures were embedded and compressed to WebP, and meshes welded, with gltf-transform (MIT). Fog, flicker, ground stains and instancing are original BURN City code (same license as the game).

Modifications and provenance

Model exports were independently prepared and optimized by Dernière Nuit à Perpignan, whose source credits identify the original Sketchfab and OpenGameArt artists, source downloads and conversions. WebP material compression, model scaling, root-motion correction, animation aliasing, gameplay-aware appearance selection and hit/death visual fallback are changes made by that project and/or BURN City. The original creator, source and license remain cited for each character.

The Ashen Cosmodrome — environment

The second Zombies map is an original BURN / XRPL launch complex. Layout, names, tram pads, and lighting are BURN City's own and are not a copy of any commercial zombies map.

  • Kenney — Space Kit 2.0 · kenney.nl/assets/space-kit · CC0 1.0
    Rocket sections, pad decks, tram car, hangars, barrels, pipes, cargo hull, satellite dish, scaffolding, and generator, vendored under assets/cosmo/. See ATTRIBUTION.md and the included license text.

gltf-transform was not required; official Kenney GLBs are already compact (about 300 KB total). Runtime tinting toward the ember palette is a BURN City change.

These attributions must remain available wherever the adapted models are displayed or redistributed. Homefront is a separate game mode, and no Homefront character models were copied into Zombies.
